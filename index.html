About Me

I'm a data professional from Harare, with a Diploma in Data Science from TelOne Centre for Learning. I turn messy, real-world data into dashboards, forecasts and tools that people actually use, whatever the industry.

My background mixes IT support, data work and web administration at Zimbabwe Open University, where I also helped rebuild and manage the university website on WordPress. I've worked in sales and customer service at OutsourceSpot Zimbabwe, and I run my own mobile device and IT equipment resale business. That gives me a practical, business-first view of data: I build analytics on real sales and stock numbers, not just practice datasets.

My projects cover retail demand forecasting, inventory analytics and predictive modelling. I'm working towards the Microsoft PL-300 Power BI Data Analyst certification, and I'm heading into BI development and data engineering.
